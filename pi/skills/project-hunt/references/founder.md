## Product taste and cash constraints

- Local-first, zero account when possible, raw export, calm UI, single-purpose
  micro-SaaS or OSS. No marketplace or gamification.
- SaaS needs a narrow job, reachable buyer, recurring pain, and a validation
  route before implementation.
- Ecommerce cash tests must be reversible and low-capital: default hypothetical
  cap €150, no inventory purchase or paid ads before a demand signal, and no
  external action without explicit user approval.
- Prefer made-to-order, print-on-demand, supplier-fulfilled, digital, or
  pre-order tests. Reject counterfeit, unsafe, ingestible/cosmetic, medical
  claims, financial, and heavily regulated products by default.

## Outcome
- `skills/shared/pr` (Claude and Pi, override `on`): read the diff, draft the
  body in a file, check it, open with `gh pr create --body-file`. Body: one
  opening line, `## What`, `## Why`, `## How to test`, in the `write-direct`
  voice transposed to English. Adapted from Matt Pocock's `pr` skill
  (`4ae2317d`, MIT): its visuals menu (Dex Horthy's `show-me`) is
  `visuals.md`, its Evidence section becomes the before/after rule of How to
  test, its Merge Danger section a one-way door bullet in Why. Credits in
  `CREDITS.md`.
- `workflow/pr-body-contract.md` carries those three rules and the template
  exception: a repo template with its own what-changed and verification
  sections stands in for ours. `workflow/agent-rules.md` points PR bodies to
  the skill; `write-direct` names `pr` as the English transposition for PRs.
- `workflow/runtime/pr-body-guard.mjs`, run by the Claude `PreToolUse` Bash
  hook `claude/hooks/pr-body-guard.mjs` and the Pi extension
  `pi/extensions/pr-body.ts`, denies a `gh pr create` without a body and a
  create or edit body missing our headings or the template's, with AI
  attribution, or with non-ASCII prose. It masks quotes, heredocs and comments,
  checks every invocation with its own body value (inline, heredoc, regular
  file after a `cd`), refuses a body built at run time, and fails open.
- ADR-0041 records the fourth Claude hook, review date 2027-02-03.

## Context
Of the owner's last 60 PRs, 44 had the three headings; 16 did not (fleet
workers with `What changed` / `Validation`, empty bodies, `Summary`). The
owner asked for every PR to open in our template, in the direct voice.

## Decisions
- Our template wins over upstream's; the upstream ideas are folded into it.
- A guard rather than text only, since fleet workers already ignored the
  contract text.
- Two cross-model review rounds (budget): round 1 BLOCK (1 P1, 11 P2, 1 P3),
  round 2 BLOCK (10 P2), all fixed with a test each. Remaining shell edge cases
  are accepted: the guard fails open and guards a convention.

## Accepted Drift
- `gh api` POSTs, a `gh pr create` nested inside a double-quoted `"$(...)"`,
  and PRs opened by scripts outside a tool call are not seen.
- A mechanical `gh pr edit --body` on a pre-contract body is refused until the
  body is rewritten.
